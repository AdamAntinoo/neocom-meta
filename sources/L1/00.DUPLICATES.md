## Goals

Holding area for duplicate or near-duplicate requirements. This is **not** a product epic. Do not run `/neo:extract-l2` on this file as a normal extract source. New product requirements belong in a numbered epic under sections with Review status starting at `to review`.

## How to resolve a block

1. **Remove** — delete or strike the holding block when the duplicate is discarded.
2. **Reinsert** — copy the requirement text back into the owning product epic at `to review` or `reviewed`, then annotate or remove this block.
3. **Keep** — leave the block here while investigating; source epic Review should stay `duplicated`.
