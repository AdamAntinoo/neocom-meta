Card lifecycle — one state per card, kept in the `State` column and on the card's button in the epic `## Sources`:

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `selected` | Active in the epic named in `Epic`. |
| `returned` | Released to the pool and re-associated to the target in `Epic` (e.g. `ASSETS`, `CACHE`, `MINING`). |
| `out of scope` | Belongs to another domain (assets/ships); reused by its target epic, not this one. |
| `deleted` | Moved to `L0/archive/`; unavailable to every epic. |
